Hello, and welcome. Today: a very short story about a lighthouse.

> sea
The lighthouse stood at the end of a long stone pier. Every night for ninety years, its lamp turned, slowly, out over the water.

> rain, bell
Then one winter night, in the middle of a storm, the lamp stopped. [sighs] The keeper climbed the stairs, all one hundred and twelve of them.

> night
He found the trouble, a single loose wire, and fixed it by hand. And the light came back on, just as a fishing boat turned for home.

That's all for today. [laughs] Thanks for listening.
